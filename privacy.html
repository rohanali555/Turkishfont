Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026. Your privacy and data security are our top priorities.

1. Overview & Commitment

Fancy Text Generator ("we", "our", "us") operates a free online Unicode text converter tool. This Privacy Policy outlines how information is handled when you access and use our website.

2. Client-Side Processing & Zero Server Logging

All text entered into our generator input box is processed entirely within your web browser using client-side JavaScript. We do not collect, transmit, store, or log any text content, usernames, or inputs you enter into the tool.

3. Local Storage

Our website utilizes web browser `localStorage` solely to remember your UI display preference (Light Mode or Dark Mode). No personal identification data is saved in local storage.

4. Cookies & Analytics

We do not use invasive tracking cookies. Standard server access logs (such as IP address, browser user-agent, and requested page URL) may be logged temporarily by hosting infrastructure for performance and security auditing only.

5. External Links

Our website may contain links to external sites or resource recommendations. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

6. Contact & Updates

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us through our official support channels. We reserve the right to update this policy as web standards evolve.
