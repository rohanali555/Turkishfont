General Questions

1. How does Fancy Text Generator work?

Fancy Text Generator maps standard ASCII keyboard characters to decorative Unicode symbols. Because these are standard Unicode characters rather than custom CSS font files, they can be copied and pasted across social media platforms like Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, and Discord.

2. Is Fancy Text Generator free to use?

Yes, our tool is 100% free with unlimited conversions, zero registration, and instant one-click copying.

3. Why do some symbols show up as boxes or question marks?

If a character displays as a blank box or question mark (often called "tofu"), it means the recipient device or older operating system does not support that specific Unicode block yet. Updating the operating system to the latest version usually restores full symbol visibility.

4. Can I use fancy text in Instagram bios and TikTok captions?

Yes! Millions of creators use fancy Unicode text to stand out on Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, Discord, Steam, and PUBG display names.

5. Does this website collect my typed text?

No. All text conversions are processed client-side inside your browser memory. We never transmit, record, or store your typed input.
